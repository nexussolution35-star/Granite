Our story

Stone is all we do —
and we do all of it.

GraniteFactory began in a single workshop unit with one belief: that a countertop is only as good as the people who template, cut and fit it. Fifteen years on, that has not changed. We still select every slab with the homeowner, still template with a laser, and still send our own crews to install.

No sales floor middlemen. No anonymous sub-contractors arriving in an unmarked van. Just one accountable team, one warranty, and a finish we sign our name to — across kitchens, islands, vanities and outdoor bars in the Riverside Metro.
