How it works

Four steps, no surprises

Every stage is scheduled, photographed and explained. You approve the slab and the price before we cut a thing.

You are in control — walk away at any step, no pressure.
1

Design & sample

We visit, measure, and bring physical slabs to match your cabinetry, light and lifestyle.

2

Laser template

Once the slab is approved we laser-template your cabinetry for a precise digital model.

3

Workshop fabrication

Your stone is cut, edged and polished in our own workshop — seams planned, cut-outs perfect.

4

Install & seal

Our crew fits, seals and hands over a spotless kitchen — usually inside a single day.
